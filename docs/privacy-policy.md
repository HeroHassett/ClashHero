# ClashHero Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 7, 2026

ClashHero ("the bot", "we", "us") is a Discord bot that helps people run Clash of Clans clans. It is
operated by the ClashHero Team (Alex Hassett). This policy explains what data the bot collects, why, how long it is
kept, and how you can see or delete it. Questions or requests: **[clashherobot@gmail.com](mailto:clashherobot@gmail.com)**.

## Summary

- We only collect what the bot's features need. We don't read your messages.
- We never sell or rent data, never use it for advertising, and never share it except as described
  below.
- You can see your data with `/privacy mydata` and delete it with `/privacy delete` at any time.
- When ClashHero is removed from a server, that server's data is deleted.
- ClashHero only uses the official Clash of Clans API. It never signs in to anyone's game account.

## 1. Data we collect

### From Discord

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Discord user ID | To remember which Clash of Clans accounts you linked, your alerts, roster signups and target calls |
| Server (guild), channel and role IDs | To store each server's settings: tracked clans, log channels, reminders, autoroles and similar |
| Server member list and members' roles | Only for servers that turn on autoroles or nickname management, to give and remove roles and set nicknames. This is read when needed and not stored, apart from roles waiting to be removed |
| What you type into commands | To carry out the command, e.g. player tags, roster names, base names and links, reminder text, target-call notes and flag reasons |
| Images you attach to `/bases add` | We store the Discord link to the image to show it on the base card |

We do **not** collect message content, voice data, or your email address, and we do not store
Discord usernames or avatars.

### From the Clash of Clans API

ClashHero reads **public** game data from Supercell's official Clash of Clans API for the clans that
servers add and the players linked to or tracked by the bot. This includes player tags and names,
Town Hall and hero levels, trophies and league, donations, war and Clan War League attacks, Clan
Capital raids, Clan Games progress and clan membership.

From this public data the bot also **works out** some information, for example:

- estimated "last seen" times and activity counts, based on changes between checks;
- estimated ranked (legend league and other tier) attacks and defenses, based on trophy changes;
- player name history and clan join/leave history;
- war, raid and season statistics.

These are estimates and may be inaccurate.

### Account verification

When you use `/verify`, you give the bot a one-time API token from the game's settings. The bot
sends it to Supercell to confirm you own the account, and then discards it. It is never stored.

## 2. How we use data

Only to provide ClashHero's features: tracking and statistics, logs and live messages, reminders and
alerts, rosters, base tracking, autoroles and nicknames, account linking, and keeping the bot secure
and working. We may look at aggregated, non-identifying usage information (such as the number of
servers) to keep the bot running well.

## 3. Who can see it

- **Members of the servers you use the bot in.** Information the bot posts in a server can be seen
  by that server's members, e.g. stats, logs, rosters and your linked accounts on a profile. Server
  managers can see and manage server data such as flags, rosters and reminders.
- **Service providers** that host and run the bot, only as needed to operate it.
- **Supercell** receives the requests the bot makes to the Clash of Clans API, such as player tags,
  and verification tokens you provide.
- **Discord** processes everything sent through its platform under
  [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
- **Legal requirements.** We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the bot, its users
  or others from harm or abuse.

We never sell, rent or trade data.

## 4. How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Linked accounts, alerts, roster signups | Until you delete them, delete your data, or the server removes the bot |
| Server settings and server-created content (rosters, flags, reminders, bases) | Until the server's managers delete it or the bot is removed from the server |
| Activity estimates (last seen, hourly activity) | 90 days |
| War, raid, legend, Clan Games, season and membership history | About 13 months |
| Cached data on players who are not linked and no longer tracked | 180 days after they were last seen |

When ClashHero is removed from a server, that server's data is deleted right away. If the bot was
offline when it was removed, the data is deleted within 7 days of the bot noticing.

## 5. Your choices and rights

- **See your data:** `/privacy mydata`.
- **Delete your data:** `/privacy delete` removes everything linked to your Discord account in every
  server. Server settings you created (such as reminders) belong to that server, so they are kept
  but no longer attributed to you.
- **Remove specific things:** `/link delete` unlinks an account; `/track remove` stops alerts;
  roster sign-ups can be withdrawn with the Opt-out button.
- **Other requests**, such as correcting data or removing game history about a player tag you own:
  email **[clashherobot@gmail.com](mailto:clashherobot@gmail.com)**. We respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights,
such as to access, correct, delete or move your data, or to object to processing. Contact us to use
them. Where the GDPR applies, we process data on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing
the features you and your server choose to use, and on your consent where you link or verify an
account.

## 6. Security

Data is stored on servers we control and protected with reasonable technical measures. No system is
perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your data, we will
notify affected users as required by law.

## 7. Children

You must meet Discord's minimum age requirement (at least 13, or older where local law requires) to
use ClashHero. We don't knowingly collect data from children below that age; if you believe we have,
contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. International users

ClashHero may store and process data in the United States or other countries where our service
providers operate.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. We'll change the effective date above and, for significant changes, post
a notice through the bot or its support server. Continuing to use ClashHero after a change means you
accept the updated policy.
