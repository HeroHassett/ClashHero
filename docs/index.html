Features

Everything a clan leader keeps track of

ClashHero watches your clans around the clock and keeps the numbers ready when you need them.

Legend & ranked tracking

Every ranked attack and defense with opponent, stars, destruction and the army used. Alerts by DM or channel, plus day, week and season logs and live leaderboards.

CWL rosters

Sign-up buttons, eligibility rules and groups, with lineups suggested from your members' real CWL and war hit rates. Automatic reminders for anyone who hasn't signed up.

War & CWL stats

Hit rates by Town Hall difference, missed attacks, defense holds, target calling, CWL standings and round-by-round history for every member.

Live logs

Joins and leaves (and where members went), donations, upgrades, super troop boosts, war attacks, raids, Clan Games and clan setting changes, posted as they happen.

Getting started

Up and running in a minute

Add ClashHero

Invite the bot to your server with the Add to Discord button.

Connect your clan

Run /setup clan with your clan tag. Tracking starts right away.

Link accounts

Members run /link create with their player tag, then check /todo and /help.

Built to be trusted

Your clan, your data

No account access

ClashHero only reads Supercell's official public API. It never asks for or signs in to anyone's game account.

Free, no paywalls

Every feature is available to every server. There are no premium tiers.

Delete anytime

See what's stored with /privacy mydata and remove it with /privacy delete. Read the privacy policy.

Ready for updates

ClashHero tells you when the Clash API is down for maintenance and picks up again on its own. Check /status anytime.
